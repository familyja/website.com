Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 July 2026

AHED GRAIN CARS S.A.R.L. (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides AG Auto & Build Tools as an offline utility app. This policy explains, in plain language, how the app handles information.

01

Information we collect

We do not collect, access, transmit, retain, sell, or share personal information or sensitive user data through the app. The app does not create user accounts and does not request Android device permissions.

02

Calculator entries and checklist choices

Numbers entered in the car budget, fuel cost, tile, paint, and concrete calculators—and choices made in the vehicle checklist—are processed only in temporary memory to show a result on your screen.

They are not uploaded, stored in a database, or made available to us. They are discarded when the relevant screen or app is closed.

03

Internet, advertising, and third parties

The app has no internet permission and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party software development kits that collect data.

We do not use the app to profile users or send marketing messages.

If you download the app through Google Play or another app store, that store may process information under its own privacy policy. That processing is separate from the app and is controlled by the store provider.

04

Children’s privacy

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed specifically to children. Because the app collects no personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

05

Data security and retention

The app’s offline design and lack of data collection substantially reduce privacy risk. Because we do not receive or store user data through the app, there is no app user-data retention period and no app-held personal data to delete.

06

Changes to this policy

If a future app version changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and the app-store data disclosures before or when that version is released. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
